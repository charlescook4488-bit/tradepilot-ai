Beginner learning centre

BUY / SELL / WAIT

A rule-based BUY or SELL means configured technical conditions aligned in sampled data. WAIT means one or more conditions failed. Neither predicts profit.

EMA, RSI and MACD

EMA smooths trends. RSI measures recent momentum. MACD compares moving averages to describe momentum changes.

ADX and ATR

ADX estimates trend strength, not direction. ATR estimates recent price movement and can frame hypothetical stop distances.

Why does free data vary?

Public APIs can be delayed, rate-limited or unavailable. Always verify source, timestamp and instrument.

Why paper trade first?

Paper trading tests rules without risking money. Simulated fills may differ from real fills.

Economic events checklist
